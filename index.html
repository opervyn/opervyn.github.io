01 / ABOUT ABLEHARBOR

A dedicated team.
Operations made easier.

ABLEHARBOR is an independent offshore operations support initiative that began in early 2026. Based in Bangladesh and built around a remote-first model, we work with businesses that need practical help with recurring operational tasks—without requiring a shared office.

Our work is focused on two service areas: Data Entry and Virtual Assistant support. We provide the people, build the dedicated team and coordinate delivery; your business remains in control of instructions, systems and workflow.

07 / QUESTIONS

Good to know
before we begin.

Some useful details about working with ABLEHARBOR.

Who does ABLEHARBOR work with?+

ABLEHARBOR supports businesses and organizations that need remote data entry or task-based virtual assistant support.

What services do you provide?+

Our current service areas are Data Entry, Virtual Assistant support, Virtual Assistant support. Each project is assessed against the client’s requirements and available capacity.

Can you support recurring projects?+

Yes. We can discuss recurring and ongoing workflows, with dedicated agents assigned according to the agreed scope and workload.

How is project capacity determined?+

Around five people are in our available team pool, and one agent is available for immediate assignment. Additional recruitment can be discussed if the project requires more people, but it proceeds only after the client confirms.

How do you handle quality?

ABLEHARBOR coordinates communication and progress, while agreed quality checks follow the client's instructions and acceptance requirements. The client defines the systems, processes and final acceptance standards.

Who provides task-specific training?

The client provides the training, instructions, examples, SOPs and authorized access needed for its specific workflow. The assigned agent practices the tasks, and the client reviews sample work and confirms readiness before regular operations begin.

Can ABLEHARBOR sign an NDA?

ABLEHARBOR is prepared to review and sign a mutually agreed Non-Disclosure Agreement before confidential project information is shared, subject to review and authorized approval. Visit our NDA & Confidentiality page for more information.

How can I request a quote?

Use the inquiry form below or email ABLEHARBOR.BUSINESS@GMAIL.COM with your service requirements, expected workload and preferred team arrangement.

Can you provide more than one dedicated agent?+

Yes, subject to availability and project scope. One agent is currently available for immediate assignment. If more capacity is needed, we discuss recruitment and obtain client confirmation before proceeding.
