CLIENT TRUST & CONFIDENTIALITY

Your business information deserves careful handling.

ABLEHARBOR understands that data entry and virtual assistant work may involve access to internal business information. We are prepared to review and sign a mutually agreed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before confidential project information is shared or access is granted, subject to authorized approval.

Our confidentiality approach

Mutually agreed NDA

The document, parties, permitted purpose, duration, exclusions and other terms are reviewed and agreed before signature.

Access based on project responsibilities

The client and ABLEHARBOR discuss which personnel need access, which systems or files are involved and what actions are permitted.

Client instructions for information handling

File-sharing methods, storage locations, communication channels, retention periods and deletion requirements are agreed during onboarding.

No public use without permission

Client-provided confidential material and business records are not intentionally used in public marketing without prior permission.
