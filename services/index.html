A CLIENT-GUIDED WORKING MODEL

How service onboarding works

1. Explain the task and expectations

You outline the responsibilities, expected output, working hours, tools and communication requirements.

2. Provide task-specific training

You train the assigned agent on your specific process and provide the relevant instructions, examples, standard operating procedures and authorized access.

3. Review practice work

The agent completes sample tasks so you can review their understanding, provide feedback and confirm readiness before regular work starts.

4. Begin and monitor regular work

Once approved, the agent follows the agreed workflow. OPERVYN coordinates communication and progress and carries out agreed checks during delivery.

Flexible remote capacity

Around five people are in OPERVYN’s available team pool, with one agent currently available for immediate assignment. Additional recruitment can be discussed for larger projects and proceeds only after client confirmation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose only one service?

Yes. You can request Data Entry, Virtual Assistant support or a combination, depending on the tasks and the agent capabilities required.

Who trains the assigned agent?

The client provides task-specific training, instructions, examples and access to the required systems. The agent practices the workflow and the client reviews sample work before regular operations begin.

Can I hire one agent or multiple agents?

Both arrangements can be discussed. Team size depends on the workload, task complexity, schedule and agreed responsibilities.

How does OPERVYN work remotely?

Our team works from home rather than from a shared office. We agree on communication, schedules, task instructions and delivery expectations with each client.

How many people are available?

Approximately five people are in our available team pool, with one agent available for immediate assignment. Any additional hiring for a project is discussed with and approved by the client first.

Can OPERVYN sign an NDA?

OPERVYN is prepared to review a mutually agreed NDA before confidential information is shared, subject to authorized approval. See our NDA & Confidentiality page for details.
